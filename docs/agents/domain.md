# Domain docs

This repository uses a single-context layout:
GLOSSARY.md at the root and decisions under docs/adr/.

Before exploring, read GLOSSARY.md and ADRs relevant to the work.
If they do not exist, proceed silently. Domain-modeling creates
them lazily as terms and decisions are resolved.

Use the glossary’s vocabulary in proposals, issues, and tests.
Surface conflicts with existing ADRs explicitly, citing the ADR
and explaining why its decision should be reconsidered.
